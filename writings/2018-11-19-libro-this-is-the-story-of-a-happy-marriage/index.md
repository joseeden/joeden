*"The quality of life is defined not by its length but by its depth."*

Book 77 of 100

![alt text](image.png)

When you have been through life's lowest valleys, you appreciate the joyous peaks so much more.

This collection of essays takes you through that spectrum from a deeply personal, honest perspective. 

The author writes about divorce, finding real love, committing to the craft of writing, friendship, and loss.

It is warm, vulnerable, and grounded. 

A wonderful read and definitely worth listening to, whether you are a woman or a man.
