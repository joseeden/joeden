*"I may have found a solution to the wife problem."*

Book 78 of 100

![alt text](image.png)

Mildly entertaining read. 

Honestly, the whole dynamic felt like fan fiction of Sheldon and Amy from *The Big Bang Theory*. 

A socially awkward genetics professor making a 16-page questionnaire to find a wife, only to get completely derailed by a spontaneous woman who meets none of his criteria.

Listening to non-stop business books gets exhausting after a while. The core ideas start repeating themselves, just repackaged with different buzzwords. 

I think I'll stick to fiction and good narratives to close out the year.
