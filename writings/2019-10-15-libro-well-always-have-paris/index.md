# Libro: We'll Always Have Paris 

*We'll Always Have Paris*, by Ray Bradbury

Book 44 of 100

“These stories were created by two people: the me who watches and the me who writes.
Both of them have lived under one sign: DON’T THINK. DO.”

![alt text](image.png)

There’s something about Bradbury’s writing that feels observant without trying too hard to explain everything.

You get these small moments, strange encounters, bits of memory, and quiet human details that somehow become stories simply because someone paid attention to them long enough.

Maybe that’s one of the lessons here.

Watch closely.

Notice things.

Write them down.

Don’t overthink every idea until there’s nothing left of it.

Sometimes the best thing you can do is just start.

Let the story figure itself out along the way.

So yeah.

**Don’t think. Do.**
