# Libro: The Martian

*The Martian*, by Andy Weir

"I'm pretty much fucked. That is my considered opinion."

![alt text](image.png)

I feel like returning to this one book is always a blast. 

If you have only seen the movie, you already know the setup where astronaut Mark Watney gets left behind on Mars after a dust storm forces an emergency evacuation.

What the movie trims down, though, is just how much math, engineering, and duct tape goes into surviving on a dead planet. Almost the entire book is made up of Mark's mission logs, detailing how he handles daily crises:

1. Fixing sudden oxygen leaks.
2. Clearing solar panels covered in fine dust.
3. Driving across kilometers of wasteland in a rover that constantly breaks down.

The thing I like about this, is that, Mark does not sit around despairing. 

He swears, figures out the physics, and starts working the problem. 

And I pretty much relate to that.
