# Libro: Project Hail Mary

*Project Hail Mary*, by Andy Weir

“I’m smart enough now to know I’m stupid. That’s progress.”

![alt text](image.png)

I will skip the heavy plot twists because going into this one blind is half the fun. 

It starts with a guy named "Ryland Grace" waking up with total amnesia on a spaceship, completely unsure of his own name, let alone why he is floating through deep space.

Gradually, through flashbacks and frantic experiments, he pieces together a horrifying reality. 

- A spaceborne microbe is eating the sun
- Earth is freezing
- He was sent on a one-way mission to find out why one single star is immune

If you loved *The Martian*, this takes that exact same formula and actually cranks it up. 

Except this time, he is not entirely alone.
